A bigger model is better everywhere

We trained whisper-medium, three times the size of whisper-small, on exactly the same 64 hours of speech as R-0007, with exactly the same recipe. Only the model size changed. On the hard exam (long, natural sentences it has never seen), letter errors fell from 10.2% to 8.0% and word errors from 44% to 38%. On the NCHLT exam, letter errors fell from 2.9% to 2.3%. It cost about $4.30, less than estimated.

EnglishNames and borrowed English words are still the biggest misses.

How training went

Faster and cheaper than planned: the smoke test measured 2.76 seconds per step, but that number included start-up time spread over only 30 steps. The real speed was 1.52 seconds per step, so the run took 2.3 hours instead of 3.7, and cost about $4.30 instead of $7.
