What goes wrong

It hears the English, then spells it like isiXhosa

One surprise: our training data already has some English phrases read by isiXhosa speakers, and R-0012 writes those perfectly. So it can write English. It fails when English sits inside fast, natural isiXhosa speech. Fast acted dialogue with background sound also hurts the isiXhosa parts.
